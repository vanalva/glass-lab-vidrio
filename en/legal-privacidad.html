Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

01 — Data we collect

At Glass Lab (hereinafter, "we") we collect personal data when you interact with our website, request quotes or contact our team. The data we may collect includes: full name, email address, telephone number, company or organisation, postal address and any additional information you voluntarily provide through contact forms.

We also automatically collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited and visit duration, using cookies and similar technologies.

02 — How we use your data

We use your personal data for the following purposes: to answer your enquiries and quote requests, to provide information about our architectural glass products and services, to improve the browsing experience on our website, to meet legal and contractual obligations, and to send you commercial communications whenever you have given prior consent.

The legal basis for processing your data is the consent you give when submitting forms, the performance of contracts or pre-contracts, and our legitimate interest in improving our services.

03 — Cookies

Our website uses first-party and third-party cookies to ensure it works correctly, to analyse web traffic and to personalise your experience. See our Cookie Policy for detailed information on the types of cookies we use, their purpose and how to manage or disable them from your browser settings.

04 — Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the data protection legislation in force in the Republic of Panama, you have the right to: access your personal data, rectify inaccurate data, request erasure of your data, object to processing, request restriction of processing and exercise the right to data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, you can contact us at the email address given in the contact section. We will respond to your request within a maximum of 30 business days.
